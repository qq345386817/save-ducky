Save Ducky

Save Ducky Ayuda

Save Ducky is about moving water, gravity, and a tiny duck. The goal is always clear: guide Ducky to the flag.

Controls

  • Rotate the tank left or right, or rotate the device on iPhone and iPad when available.
  • Use the water buttons to raise or drain the water.
  • Drag inside the tank on touch devices to set the water level directly.
  • Later levels may include valve and plug controls for slower water changes.

Puzzle tips

  • Look at the flag, blockers, and current water level before rotating.
  • Small water changes often matter more than filling the whole tank.
  • If Ducky is stuck, restart the level and try the rotation first.

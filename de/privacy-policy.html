Save Ducky Datenschutz

Save Ducky is designed as a local puzzle game. It does not require an account and does not require a gameplay network connection.

Data collection

The app does not collect account details, gameplay profiles, or personal cloud data for normal play.

Optional microphone and speech features

Optional sound controls may request microphone and speech recognition permission. Those permissions are used only for the local control feature you enable.
